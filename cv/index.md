## Research experience

My current work concerns machine-learning calibration and pile-up classification for signals in the forward ATLAS calorimeter, followed through to jet and hadronic-recoil performance. My doctoral work centered on top-quark-pair production with an associated photon, including differential cross-sections, effective-field-theory interpretations, and data-driven background estimates.

Earlier projects covered ATLAS pixel data-acquisition and byte-stream software, boosted-jet substructure and top tagging, and cosmic-ray anisotropy with AMS-02. The [research section]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) explains each strand in more detail.
