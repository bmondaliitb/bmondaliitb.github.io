Thought Breaker organizes ideas as connected notes on a zoomable canvas. Notes can be placed freely, linked with directional arrows, and marked todo, doing, done, or blocked. The graph shows dependencies and what can be worked on next.

## Interface and storage

Built with Python, GTK4, and Libadwaita, the app supports panning, zooming, selection, dragging, and drawing connections. It also runs on macOS and can be packaged as an application bundle.

Projects are stored as readable Markdown.
