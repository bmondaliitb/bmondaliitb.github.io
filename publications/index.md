## Top quarks and photons

### Measurements of inclusive and differential cross-sections of tt̄γ production

**ATLAS Collaboration**, *Journal of High Energy Physics* **10** (2024) 191.

Using 140 fb⁻¹ of proton–proton collision data at √s = 13 TeV, this work measures inclusive and differential cross-sections in single-lepton and dilepton channels. The photon transverse-momentum spectrum is also interpreted in effective field theory.

[Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP10(2024)191) · [Open data and likelihoods](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.146899)

### Projected sensitivity to top-quark couplings at the HL-LHC

**ATLAS Collaboration**, ATL-PHYS-PUB-2025-015 (2025).

This projection extrapolates Run 2 measurements of tt̄γ and tt̄Z to high-luminosity scenarios and studies the expected sensitivity to electroweak top-quark couplings in an effective-field-theory framework.

[CERN record](https://cds.cern.ch/record/2927962) · [ATLAS public note and figures](https://atlas.web.cern.ch/Atlas/GROUPS/PHYSICS/PUBNOTES/ATL-PHYS-PUB-2025-015/)

### Charge asymmetry in tt̄γ production

**ATLAS Collaboration**, *Physics Letters B* **843** (2023) 137848.

The measurement probes the charge asymmetry in top-quark-pair production with an associated photon, a process with a distinctive sensitivity to the dynamics of top-quark production.

[Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2023.137848)
