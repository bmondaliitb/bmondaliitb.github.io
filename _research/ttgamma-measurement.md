Top quarks interact with the photon through their electric charge and electroweak dipole structure. Producing a top-quark pair together with a high-energy photon gives a direct way to test that interaction at the energy frontier.

## The measurement

My doctoral research contributed to the ATLAS measurement of inclusive and differential tt̄γ cross-sections using 140 fb⁻¹ of proton–proton collision data collected at √s = 13 TeV. The analysis treats single-lepton and dilepton decays of the top-quark pair and measures distributions describing the photon, leptons, jets, and their angular relationships.

The result was unfolded to a particle-level fiducial phase space. This removes the leading effects of detector acceptance and resolution while keeping the measurement close enough to the experimental selection to limit model dependence. The measured distributions were found to agree with the Standard Model simulations within their uncertainties.

## From distributions to interactions

The high-energy tail of the photon transverse-momentum distribution is particularly sensitive to modifications of the top–photon interaction. We used this information to constrain electroweak dipole operators in Standard Model effective field theory, including a combination with the photon and Z-boson spectra in tt̄Z production.

The published numerical results and statistical likelihoods are preserved in [HEPData](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.146899), making the measurement reusable for comparisons and future interpretations.
