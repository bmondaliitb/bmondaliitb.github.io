## Measurement

My doctoral research contributed to ATLAS measurements of inclusive and differential tt̄γ cross-sections using 140 fb⁻¹ of proton–proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV. The analysis covers single-lepton and dilepton decays, measuring photon, lepton, and jet kinematics and their angular relationships.

The distributions were unfolded to a particle-level fiducial phase space to correct for detector acceptance and resolution while limiting model dependence. They agree with Standard Model simulations within uncertainties.

## Top–photon interaction

Associated photon production probes the top quark's electric charge and electroweak dipole structure. We used the high-energy photon transverse-momentum spectrum to constrain electroweak dipole operators in Standard Model effective field theory, including a combination with photon and Z-boson spectra in tt̄Z production.
