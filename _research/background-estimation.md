## Electrons reconstructed as photons

An electron can be reconstructed as a photon when its track is not correctly associated with its calorimeter cluster. I studied this background in tt̄γ events, using electron-enriched control samples to estimate the misidentification probability.

## Non-prompt leptons

Leptons from heavy-flavor decays, photon conversions, or misidentified hadrons can pass the signal selection. I estimated their rates and kinematic distributions using loose and tight lepton selections, with efficiencies measured in data.

Both estimates were used in the differential tt̄γ measurement. Validation covered control-region purity, transfer to the signal region, and systematic uncertainties from differences in event composition.
